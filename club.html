A CLUB WITH A LONG MEMORY

Founded in Madrid in 1902, Real Madrid has become one of football's most decorated clubs. Its home, the Santiago Bernabéu, sits in the heart of the Spanish capital and has hosted generations of unforgettable matches.

The club competes in La Liga and plays its home fixtures at the Bernabéu. Its history spans domestic titles, European competition and an enduring connection with supporters around the world.
